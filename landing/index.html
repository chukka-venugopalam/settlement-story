Why this isn't a weekend toy

The specifics matter. Here's what makes this real.

The LLM never computes a number

Every currency figure in the waterfall comes from waterfall_core.py — a pure function, locked and verified. The LLM only narrates what's already been computed. Hallucinated math in fintech isn't a minor bug; it's a trust-destroying failure.

net_settled = round(after_refunds - reserve_held, 2)

Locked and independently tested

The calculation module is never regenerated from a prompt. It's proven against 10 real settlement batches modeled on Razorpay's actual published fee structure. Run test_waterfall.py yourself before trusting this demo.

$ python test_waterfall.py
ALL PASSED (10/10 fixtures)

PDF pipeline handles real input

Settlement PDFs in the wild are messy — no consistent formatting, no clean item rows. Our extraction doesn't guess; it reproduces an exact verified figure from a deliberately messy real PDF and proves it against the calculation logic.

Expected: ₹47,299.70
Extracted: ₹47,299.70 ✓

WCAG verified, not eyeballed

Every color pairing was verified against strict WCAG relative luminance math across our red-and-white canvas. Primary brand red and ink text exceed 7.9:1 and 16.8:1 on linen paper; deduction and net settled figures maintain full AA/AAA compliance. No "this looks fine on my monitor" assumptions.
